Two boards on one shelf

Every game now builds for the PicoSystem and the Tufty 2350, which means a card has two binaries to offer instead of one. Three ways it could, drawn with the real gallery CSS. Rule 12 says a card is a picture, a name, a line about it, and a way in, so the question is how little can be added and still be honest.

Option A: three across

One row, the download split into two short board names. Play keeps the space it has; the two boards take the rest. Nothing wraps, nothing grows in height, and a device-only game just loses the Play button and keeps both downloads.

Option C: the shelf stays a shelf

The card does not change at all. .uf2 keeps pointing at the PicoSystem build, and the game's own page grows a downloads section listing both boards with a line each about which is which. Rule 12's actual words: anything longer than the blurb belongs on the game's own page.

The cost: a device-only game on a Tufty is now two clicks and a page away, and the front page stops admitting the second board exists.

Recommendation: A. It is the only one that keeps a card one row of actions tall while still telling the truth on the front page, and the shelf stays rectangular because no card changes height. B is more readable and costs height on all twenty-odd cards to say something most visitors do not need. C is the most faithful to rule 12 as written, but it hides a whole board from the shelf, and the reason the rule exists is to keep cards short rather than to keep them uninformative.
