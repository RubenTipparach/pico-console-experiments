Pico Space Program // screen mockup

Six screens at the console's native 120x120, drawn at 2.2x. One continuous flight: off the pad, into orbit, across to a moon, down on it. Two views share the flight, a 3D one out of the window and a 2D map.

CONTROLS

up / downthrottle up, throttle down
left / rightturn the nose
Astage: drop the spent booster, light the next engine
Btime warp, x1 / x10 / x50 / x200, throttle has to be shut
Xmap view
Yattitude hold: off / prograde / retrograde

WHAT IS ON SCREEN, AND WHY

  • Fuel bar, top left. The tank of the stage that is burning, not the whole rocket. Empty is the cue to press A.
  • Altitude, top right. Metres under 10 km, kilometres over.
  • Speed, bottom left. Metres per second, and it is the number that turns green when the ship is slow enough to touch down.
  • AP, bottom right. The top of the arc the ship is on. This is how orbit is read: raise AP clear of the air, then burn at AP until PE follows it up. It appears once the ship is above the air and nowhere else, because on the pad it means nothing.
  • The ring around the ship. A dot for where the ship is going (prograde) and a hollow one for the other way. Turning the nose onto the dot is the whole of flying this, so it sits on the ship rather than in a corner.
  • Nothing names a button. Rule 9. The card in the gallery lists them, the screen does not.
