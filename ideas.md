# Game Ideas

## Picomon

A top-down 3D adventure game about walking around and capturing Picomon, inspired by Pokémon.

- Challenge gym trainers
- Battle strangers on the street
- Ask someone out on a romantic date

## Pico Space Program

A tiny, simple rocket launch simulation game where you launch rockets and try to land on the many moons of Picopiter.

## Pom Lander

A demake of the hit game Tom Lander, set on Mars, the red planet.

- Pom tries to make his daily wages by lifting cargo
- Land cargo on landing pads with limited fuel
- A slowly breaking down bucket of bolts, your ship degrades over time
