Objective

Cast into deep water for the rarer fish, then set the hook's depth: surface feeders ride high and the heavy ones sit on the bottom. Wear a fish down once it takes, reeling while the line is safe and easing off when the meter climbs.

Controls 1/2

  • hold ADpower, release: cast
  • ADhook, then hold to reel
  • up↑raise the lure in the water
  • down↓drop it toward the bottom

Controls 2/2

  • left/right← →wiggle the rod
  • BSrecall the lure
  • YArecords
